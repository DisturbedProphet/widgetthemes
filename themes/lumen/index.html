Lumen

Soft white cards on a bright, airy desk. Lumen is a minimal theme: quiet type and clean panels, one of 30 in WidgetThemes, the free desktop widget app for Mac, Windows and Linux. Its dark partner is Slate, and WidgetThemes can switch between them with your system appearance.

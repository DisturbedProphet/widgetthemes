Soft widget theme

Soft UI: panels pressed out of one pale surface, inset dials, one periwinkle accent. Soft is a minimal theme: quiet type and clean panels, one of 43 widget themes in WidgetThemes, the free desktop widget app for Mac, Windows and Linux. It comes in light and dark; in Auto it follows your system's appearance.

Privacy

Your desktop stays yours.

WidgetThemes has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It doesn't send anything about you to the developer. Everything below happens on your computer, except where it says otherwise. The Mac app and the Windows and Linux app differ in places, and this page says where.

What leaves your computer

  • Weather. To show the forecast, the app asks Open-Meteo for weather at the city you typed in Settings, or at your location rounded to about one kilometre if the system allows the app to know it. Without either, the Windows and Linux app uses the city your time zone is named after. A city's name is sent to Open-Meteo's place search. Open-Meteo sees your IP address, as any web server does. To name the place, the Mac app asks macOS, which uses Apple's geocoding service, and the Windows and Linux app asks OpenStreetMap's Nominatim with the rounded location, at most every 15 minutes. This only happens if you add a Weather or Moon & Sun widget.
  • Calendar links (Windows and Linux). If you paste calendar links into Settings, the app downloads them from wherever they're hosted, such as Google or Outlook, about every ten minutes.
  • Album art (Mac). When Spotify is playing, the cover image is downloaded from Spotify's own image server, using the address Spotify provides.

Nothing else is sent anywhere.

What the app reads, and only on your computer

  • Calendars and Reminders (Mac), if you add the Events or Reminders widget and allow access. Ticking a reminder off marks it complete in Reminders. On Windows and Linux, reminders are kept in the app itself.
  • Location, if you allow it, to find local weather: from Location Services on a Mac, Windows' location service, or GeoClue on Linux. You can type a city instead.
  • What's playing. On a Mac, Music and Spotify, through macOS Automation. On Windows, the system's media controls; on Linux, MPRIS. The app reads the title, artist and position, and sends play, pause and skip.
  • System readings: CPU, memory, storage, battery, network rates, the busiest apps, and connected wireless devices with their battery levels.
  • Activity, if you add the Activity widget: how long the computer was in use each hour today, how far the pointer moved and how many clicks there were. It never records keystrokes, what you click on, or which apps you use. The counts reset each day.
  • Photos from a folder you choose for the Photo widget.

Settings, layouts, saved themes, focus-timer counts and today's activity totals stay on your computer: in the app's preferences on a Mac, and in a settings file in the app's own folder on Windows and Linux. Exported theme files contain only layouts and colours, never your photos, calendars or location.

This website

This site uses no cookies and no analytics. Its headline font is loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

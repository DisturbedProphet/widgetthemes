Thanks and licences.

WidgetThemes is built in Swift and SwiftUI on Mac, and with Tauri and Rust on Windows and Linux. Its themes, widgets, decorations and drawn wallpapers are original designs made for the app. These are the outside sources it uses.

Weather data

Weather forecasts are provided by Open-Meteo.com under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. WidgetThemes is free and uses Open-Meteo's free, non-commercial API.

Generated wallpapers

Many theme wallpapers and the bonus wallpapers were created with Meta's Muse Image model and then selected, checked and edited for the app.

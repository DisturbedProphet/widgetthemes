Thanks and licences.

DeskWidgets is built in Swift and SwiftUI. Its themes, widgets, decorations and drawn wallpapers are original designs made for the app. These are the outside sources it uses.

Weather data

Weather forecasts are provided by Open-Meteo.com under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. DeskWidgets is free and uses Open-Meteo's free, non-commercial API.

Generated wallpapers

Many theme wallpapers and the bonus wallpapers were created with Meta's Muse Image model and then selected, checked and edited for the app.

Quotes

The Quote of the Day widget quotes writers whose words are in the public domain. Its word-of-the-day definitions were written for the app.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No trial, no subscription and no account.

Will it slow my Mac down?

Widgets update a few times a second at most and animate at a low frame rate. Animation pauses whenever widgets are covered by windows, and you can switch it off entirely in Settings.

What permissions does it ask for, and why?

Only what a widget you add needs: Calendars for Events, Reminders for Reminders, Location for local weather (or type a city instead), and Automation to read and control Music or Spotify. Each one is requested the first time that widget appears, and you can decline it.

Where do the widgets sit: behind my windows or on top?

Your choice. By default they sit on the desktop, behind your windows. You can raise all of them, or pin individual widgets above your windows.

Why install with a Terminal command?

macOS shows a warning for apps downloaded in a browser unless the developer pays for Apple's developer programme. WidgetThemes is free and isn't registered, so the Terminal command is the smoothest way in: it downloads the app over a secure connection, checks it matches the published checksum, and installs it. If you'd rather not use Terminal, the disk image works too, with one extra click the first time.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit WidgetThemes from its menu bar icon (right-click, then Quit) and move it from Applications to the Trash. To remove its settings as well, delete its file in ~/Library/Preferences; its name contains "WidgetThemes".

Can I use my own wallpaper?

Of course. Every theme looks good on its own wallpaper, but you can add any image under Wallpapers, or shuffle between them on a schedule.
